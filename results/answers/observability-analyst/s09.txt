### Alert Rules & Alerting Status in Grafana / Cluster

In this Amazon EKS cluster, Grafana is connected to the in-cluster Prometheus (`http://prometheus-kube-prometheus-prometheus.monitoring:9090`), which evaluates alert rules defined via Prometheus Operator (`PrometheusRule` custom resources). There are no native Grafana-managed alert rules configured; alerting is driven by Prometheus.

---

### 1. Alert Rule Groups Configured

There are **30 `PrometheusRule` rule definitions** configured in the cluster (in the `monitoring` namespace) providing standard Kubernetes, node, and Prometheus alerting coverage:

* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-kubernetes-apps` (Deployment rollouts, replica mismatches, crashlooping, pod readiness)
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-kubernetes-resources` (CPU/Memory overcommit, resource saturation)
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-kubernetes-system` (System daemon issues)
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-kubernetes-system-apiserver` & `kubernetes-system-kubelet`
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-node-exporter` & `node.rules` (Node filesystem, disk, network, memory)
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-prometheus` (Prometheus health, alertmanager connectivity)
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-alertmanager.rules`
* `prometheus-kube-prometheus-kube-state-metrics`
